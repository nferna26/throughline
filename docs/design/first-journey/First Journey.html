The curated shelves are hand-built, a few books each, where the editorial blurb is authored by hand. Search and any all-books view show cover, title, and author only, never a fabricated blurb, because the data has none. Covers work everywhere (they're generated from title and author). The generic filter pills are gone: the calm labeled shelves are the navigation, like the rows in a good bookshop.

Your reading pace

new · the only added question

The one calm choice, asked once as you start your first book. Framed in reading terms, not minutes, a few pages, a chapter, or a long sitting, so it never becomes a timer and never contradicts Today's promise of "no clock but your own." It quietly sizes the daily portion, has a sensible default (a chapter), can be skipped, and lives in Settings afterward. No goal, no streak, no target.

Connective tissue & the no-clock reconciliation

The cover is the thread. The same cloth object appears on the front door, as a small spine on the browse shelves, full-size on the chosen screen, and as the title on Today. The reader's eye follows one book the whole way.

Pace is in reading terms, never minutes. "A few pages / a chapter / a long read" sizes the daily portion behind the scenes; the reader never sees a countdown, and Today still says "no clock but your own." The pace described in the brief as 10 / 25 / 60 minutes maps to these three, but is never shown as time.

It's one question, never a funnel. Asked once, at the moment it's useful (starting the first book), with a sensible default (a chapter) and a skip ("I'll decide as I go"). Pace also lives in Settings and can be changed anytime; a returning reader who already set it skips this step entirely and lands straight on Today.

Clear of the Matter anti-patterns: no streak, goal, percentage, or quiz; no account or paywall anywhere in the path; reading is always free, and the covers do the inviting.

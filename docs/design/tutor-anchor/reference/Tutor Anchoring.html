Throughline · reader · CORE-1158

The tutor, anchored to the selection

The answer should appear in the margin beside the line you selected, the way a comment sits beside a paragraph, not at the top of the column where you have to scroll to find it. The reader's place is sacrosanct: the card comes to the reader, the text never moves. Same card, same brand; this pass fixes its placement and its states.

Active vs kept

Active = full anchored card with a hairline leader to its highlight. Kept = a compact margin marker, tinted to the highlight.

Off-screen

A small stacked "bucket" at the margin's top or bottom edge with a count. Click to jump.

Define

A small inline popover at the selection, not a margin card. A glance, not a lesson.

The tie

Matched highlight tint plus a thin leader line, on the active card only. Calm, not a hard connector.

Short vs long, and streaming

height + stream

A short answer is only as tall as its content, no chrome. A long answer caps at a max height and scrolls inside the card, never taller than the viewport, never pushing the column. While streaming, the card is fixed and the answer fills in beside the still passage.

Tutor · Context

Thoreau opens Walden with a critique of how most people live: trapped by property, debt, and routine they never chose. "Quiet desperation" names a despair without drama, hidden under ordinary busyness and amusements.

He is setting up the book's wager: that a deliberate, simplified life at the pond can reveal what living actually requires.

Define: a light inline popover

glance, not a lesson

A one-word or short selection asking Define gets a small popover right at the selection, not a margin card. It's distinct, lighter, and dismisses on the next click. Explain and Context still open the fuller margin card.

8

Narrow window: inline, below the paragraph

one component, two renders

When the window is too narrow for a readable margin card, the same component renders inline, directly below the selected paragraph. Everything above stays put, so the reader's place holds; the text below simply flows down. Truly tiny windows could fall back to a bottom sheet, but the inline expansion covers the calm common case.

b

Behavior notes for the build

scroll · stream · focus

The reader's place is sacrosanct

Reveal: if the selected line is in view, the card appears with no scroll. The card aligns its top to the selection's top, then nudges up only as far as needed to fit fully in the margin viewport (never enough to feel like a jump). If the selection is partly off-screen, the card pins to the nearest edge rather than forcing a scroll.

Stream: the card reserves its anchored position before the first token, then grows downward in place. No reflow of the column, no upward drift of the card. Past max-height it scrolls internally.

Collision: one active card; others collapse to markers. The layout never stacks two full cards, and a marker never overlaps the active card (push markers to their line, clamp within the margin, gather the overflow into the bucket).

Focus & a11y: focus stays in the reading text on reveal, the card is reachable by keyboard but not focus-grabbed. The answer region is aria-live="polite". Honor reduced-motion: no stream animation, the answer renders pre-resolved. The highlight-to-card tie is conveyed by tint and the leader, never by color alone (the active highlight also carries a ring).
